01 · GETTING STARTED

What PumpWatch does

PumpWatch is a Solana-first market-intelligence and paper-evaluation app. It watches supported launch and trade feeds, builds rolling activity measurements, evaluates potential opportunities with two separate models, checks security and market-data conditions, and records simulated trades for review.

Paper mode only. PumpWatch does not connect to a wallet, store private keys, sign blockchain transactions or execute live trades. A paper result is a simulation, not a promise that the same fill would be available in the market.

The workflow

  1. Discover: launch and market providers supply tokens to the candidate universe.
  2. Measure: price, volume, buying activity, liquidity and other features are refreshed.
  3. Assess: Ignition and Momentum independently evaluate their entry conditions, with risk gates.
  4. Simulate: if automatic paper trading is enabled and every required condition passes, the engine may open a virtual position.
  5. Review: inspect open positions, closed trades and model outcomes. Wait for a meaningful sample before drawing conclusions.
02 · ORIENTATION

Dashboard and navigation

The main app is organised into tabs along the navigation bar:

  • Overview — connection status, feed activity, model signal counts, live activity radar and recent launches.
  • Ignition — Model 01 eligibility gates, recent diagnostics and Ignition signals.
  • Momentum — Model 02 eligibility gates, recent diagnostics and Momentum signals.
  • Token Explorer — searchable candidate list with filters, sorting, token detail drill-down and eligible manual paper entry.
  • Paper trading — open virtual positions, unrealised P&L, realised P&L and position close controls.
  • Performance — completed trade and signal outcome summaries.
  • Settings — automatic paper-trading controls and discovery switch.

The top status indicator reports the feed state. The header also labels the app as PAPER MODE so simulated activity is not confused with live execution.

03 · DATA & DISCOVERY

Live feed and discovery

The Overview page shows whether the live Solana feed is connected, when the last event arrived, the number of trade events received and rejected, and active token subscriptions. The current free Shrine feed has a maximum of 50 active token subscriptions and is not whole-market coverage.

Live activity radar

Shows tokens ranked by recent one-minute net buying, with buyer counts and buy/sell value ratio. It is an activity view, not automatically a buy signal. A token can appear here while failing one or more model or risk gates.

Latest launches

Shows recent launch events received by the configured feed. A launch appearing here means it was discovered; it does not mean it has passed security, liquidity or entry checks.

Discovery scan

Discovery runs in the background when enabled. In Token Explorer, Run discovery scan starts an additional scan immediately. The status text reports the latest scan's discoveries, evaluations and passed candidates. Candidates without sufficiently fresh and complete market data are excluded from the fresh leaderboard.

Provider coverage, rate limits and subscription caps affect what PumpWatch can see. “No signals” can simply mean no token has met all the required conditions during the observed period.
04 · SIGNAL MODELS

Ignition and Momentum

The models are intentionally different. They should be assessed separately rather than treated as two names for the same signal.

Ignition · Model 01 — early build-up

Looks for early accumulation before a move becomes overextended. The displayed baseline gates include:

3+ trades / 1m
3+ unique buyers
Buyer & trade acceleration
$100+ net buy / 1m
5m price move ≤12%
Security + liquidity pass

Momentum · Model 02 — confirmed participation

Looks for stronger sustained participation and positive flow. The displayed baseline gates include:

10+ trades / 5m
Buy/sell ratio ≥1.25×
5+ unique buyers
$250+ net buy / 5m
Price move 1–18%
Security + liquidity pass

On each model page, Eligibility gates describes the rule set. Live diagnostics show recent observed tokens and how many measured gates pass. The signal list shows active or historical model signals where available.

A token may pass some gates and fail others. Security, liquidity and fresh market-data checks are fail-closed: if required evidence is missing or stale, the system should not treat the token as eligible.
05 · FINDING TOKENS

Token Explorer

Token Explorer is the candidate workbench. It presents the current fresh candidate leaderboard, not every token on Solana.

Search and filters

  • Search accepts a token symbol, name or full mint address.
  • Candidate type filters All candidates, New launches or Established.
  • Sort orders by Highest score, Highest 5m volume or Highest liquidity.
  • Run discovery scan requests a fresh discovery pass; the status line reports progress and the last scan summary.

Reading a candidate card

Each card shows the token symbol and name, scanner score out of 100, price, five-minute volume, liquidity, model/candidate label, data refresh status and up to two rejection reasons. A chart link opens the external chart when one is available.

Open a token

  1. Tap or click anywhere on the main body of a token card (the card is keyboard accessible too).
  2. The Token Intelligence detail sheet opens and refreshes data for that mint.
  3. Review market metrics, model assessments, live activity and risk/security evidence.
  4. Use Copy mint to copy the full address, or Open DexScreener chart to inspect the external chart.
  5. Close the detail sheet with the × button, tap outside the sheet, or press Escape on a keyboard.
The small Paper buy action is separate from opening the card. It is enabled only when the candidate is passed and its refresh status is fresh; the server checks freshness and safety again before creating a simulated position.

07 · SIMULATED EXECUTION

Paper trading

Paper trading records virtual entries and exits using market prices. No funds move and no blockchain transaction is submitted.

Automatic entries

When enabled, the V4 paper engine looks for fresh active Ignition or Momentum signals that are enabled in Settings. It also requires a passing security assessment, passing liquidity gate, fresh market data, minimum configured liquidity, acceptable risk score, no existing open position in the token and no active re-entry cooldown. Maximum position count and daily loss limits can also pause new entries.

Manual paper entry

In Token Explorer, use Paper buy on an eligible candidate card or in its detail panel. The app checks that the candidate passed, the safety checks passed, its evaluation is recent and market data is fresh. If the button is disabled or the server rejects the request, refresh the scan and inspect the token's detail panel before trying again.

Open positions and closing

The Paper trading tab lists open positions with entry price, current price, stake, remaining position and unrealised/realised P&L. Choose Close position to simulate closing the remaining position at a fresh available market price. A close is blocked if a fresh price cannot be obtained.

Simulated execution does not model every real-world effect such as transaction inclusion, slippage, priority fees, failed swaps or liquidity disappearing between observations. Paper performance can differ materially from live outcomes.

Settings explained

Open Settings from the main navigation. Change values and press Save paper strategy; the saved configuration is applied by the server.

Discovery engine

Background discovery enabled controls periodic candidate discovery. The Save discovery settings button saves this switch. Keep it enabled if you want the candidate universe to refresh automatically.

The minimum liquidity and maximum risk score are additional entry controls. Core safety requirements — available security pass, liquidity pass and fresh market data — cannot be switched off.
09 · LEARNING FROM RESULTS

Performance

The Performance tab shows closed paper positions, indicative signal outcomes and a recent trade history. The current UI deliberately avoids claiming a statistical edge from a tiny sample. It flags fewer than 30 closed trades as an early sample.

Review Ignition and Momentum separately: their entry logic and market conditions differ. Look at completed trade count, win rate, profit factor and drawdown once model-separated reporting is available and the sample is large enough. Signal outcome returns are indicative mark-to-market observations, not guaranteed executable fills.

A few winning or losing trades do not establish that a strategy works or fails. Keep the app in paper mode while collecting and reviewing a meaningful sample.
10 · TROUBLESHOOTING

Common questions

Why are there no signals?

Signals are selective. A token must meet the model's activity thresholds and pass security, liquidity and freshness checks. Check feed status, live activity, model diagnostics and rejection reasons. Do not loosen safety checks just to make a signal appear.

Feed says CONNECTING or no live events

Check the feed status on Overview, last event time, active subscriptions and whether the provider credential is configured on the server. The 50-subscription free-feed limit means coverage is selective. A connected feed can still have no qualifying trades.

Token Explorer is empty

Check that background discovery is enabled in Settings, then use Run discovery scan. Wait for the scan status to update. Provider errors, incomplete market data or stale snapshots can prevent a token from appearing in the fresh leaderboard.

Why is Paper buy disabled?

The candidate may not have passed its checks, its market snapshot may not be fresh, or it may no longer have a recent evaluation. Run a fresh scan and inspect the token detail panel. The server revalidates before opening a position.

Why did an automatic paper trade not open?

Confirm the master switch and the relevant model switch are on. Then check the active signal, security status, liquidity, risk score, market freshness, open-position limit, daily loss limit and re-entry cooldown.

Why can't I close a position?

Manual close requires a fresh market price. If the market provider is stale, rate-limited or unavailable, retry after a fresh price is available.

Why do values show Unknown or —?

The relevant provider may not supply that field, the token may not have enough observed trades, or the data may be incomplete. Missing evidence is displayed as unavailable rather than assumed.

10 · P0 PERFORMANCE

Signal forward testing

Each V4 signal is followed after creation. PumpWatch records the first available market observation at or after 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 60m, 4h and 24h. The Performance panel groups these observations by model.

These are mark-to-market price observations, not guaranteed fills. They do not include execution costs and can be delayed if the market-data provider is unavailable or a token has no fresh market snapshot.

Average return is the mean of recorded percentage moves. Win rate is the proportion of measured observations above zero. Always check the sample count beside each statistic. A small sample is descriptive, not evidence of a durable trading edge.

11 · P0 SIMULATION

Estimated paper execution costs

Paper entries and exits use an estimated adverse fill rather than assuming every trade executes at the displayed mid-price. The model applies configured base slippage plus a liquidity-impact component based on simulated order size relative to reported pool liquidity. It also deducts configured fees from realised P&L.

Slippage (bps)Basis points: 100 bps = 1%. Applied adversely on both entry and exit, with additional impact when simulated order size is large relative to liquidity.
Fees (bps)Estimated fee allowance is deducted across entry and exit. This is a configurable simulation assumption, not a live quote from a particular DEX.
Net realised P&LClosed-position results subtract estimated entry fees, exit fees and adverse fills. Unrealised P&L remains an indicative mark against the latest available price.
12 · P1 INTELLIGENCE

Wallet-flow quality

Token detail includes additional measurements derived from observed trade events: top-three buyer share, repeat-buyer trade share, buyer breadth and same-slot buy share. Same-slot concentration is a burst heuristic based on the provider's block/slot metadata; it is not proof of a coordinated bundle or sniper group. The V4 models also track liquidity between recent market samples and temporarily block signals after a sharp observed withdrawal (20% or more within five minutes).

This is trade-flow analysis, not a complete holder graph. It cannot prove that wallets are independent, identify common funding sources, establish beneficial ownership or replace deployer and holder-history research.
13 · P1 STRATEGY EXPERIMENTATION

Strategy presets

Settings include Balanced, Early Entry and Conservative starting configurations. Selecting a preset loads suggested stake, liquidity, risk, stop, target, trailing-stop, hold-time and cooldown values into the form. Press Save paper strategy to apply them.

The current MVP runs one shared paper configuration and one shared portfolio. Presets are sequential configurations, not simultaneous isolated portfolios. Positions record the preset active when they were opened so performance can be grouped later. Avoid comparing presets until each has a reasonable sample and comparable market conditions.

Buy/sell ratioRatio of buy value to sell value. A higher ratio indicates stronger buy-side value in that window, not certainty of continuation.
